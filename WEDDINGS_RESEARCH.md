## 1. Executive Research Summary

Maya Garh Pushkar is an ultra-exclusive fortress sanctuary located on Bhagwanpura Road in Pushkar, Rajasthan, framed by the ancient Aravalli mountain range. Developed and operated within the **Maya Luxury** portfolio, the retreat comprises six palatial private-pool villas (*Maha Maya*, *Amanjena*, *Malak*, *Adiva*, *Ameera*, and *Mayan*), monumental stone bastions, central courtyard grounds, an elevated infinity pool overlooking the valley, and authentic indoor and outdoor dining spaces.

### The Destination Wedding Opportunity
Destination weddings in Rajasthan are globally celebrated for their royal grandeur, architectural romance, and cultural immersion. However, the majority of Rajasthan wedding venues operate as high-volume commercial banquet hotels hosting hundreds or thousands of transient guests in generic ballrooms.

Maya Garh Pushkar offers a fundamentally different and rarer luxury proposition:
* **The Private Royal Fort Estate**: An intimate, private fort sanctuary where the entire estate can be taken over in an exclusive buyout.
* **Architectural Authenticity**: Hand-carved stone arches, soaring sandstone ramparts, jharokha balconies, timber-beamed ceilings, and atmospheric courtyards lit by flickering lanterns and flaming torches.
* **Intimate Royal Hospitality**: Designed for bespoke, curated gatherings where the couple and their guests experience the intimacy and privilege of living like royal hosts in their own private fortress.
* **Continuity with Desert Heritage**: Elevated pool deck, sunset terraces, palm-fringed stone pathways, and proximity to sacred Thar desert dunes for private pre-wedding rituals.
